## Context

**Thirty North** is my latest shipped product: a GST/HST threshold tracker for Canadian freelancers who sell to US and foreign clients. The core problem is timing — foreign-client work can still count toward Canada’s C$30,000 small-supplier tests even when people believe the service is zero-rated. Missing the window changes registration obligations.

Live product: [thirtynorthgst.ca](https://thirtynorthgst.ca/)

## Overview

I designed and built the full product surface end to end: private in-browser tracker, bilingual EN/FR experience, educational guides, and a product library (free tracker + paid Action Kit / Quarterly Workspace / bundle) with clear legal and delivery boundaries.

The product philosophy is intentional limits: it calculates what you enter; it does not classify your work or replace individualized tax advice.

## What shipped

- **Dual-threshold reading**: single-quarter and four-quarter C$30,000 tests with a live estimate UI
- **Private by default**: entries stay in the browser — no account, no invoice upload for the free tracker
- **Operational workspace**: add supplies, classify rows, import CSV, export/download review artifacts
- **Trust framing**: “based on CRA guidance,” review dates, explicit exclusions, and merchant-of-record checkout via Lemon Squeezy
- **Content system**: guides that explain the rule freelancers miss (worldwide taxable supplies, including many zero-rated exports)
- **Commercial layer**: Action Kit and offline quarterly workspace as paid workflow products layered on the free tool
- **Bilingual**: full EN-CA / FR-CA alternate routes and locale handling

## Stack & craft

Modern React/Vite RSC-style app with strong product IA: hero → insight strip → interactive tool → packaged offers → educational limits → guides. Schema.org organization markup, analytics consent, skip links, and policy pages (delivery, refunds, privacy, terms) treat trust as product infrastructure.

## Why it belongs on this site

Thirty North is the clearest current proof of how I ship: **ideation → durable product → honest constraints → mass-usable deployment**. Same muscles as institutional platforms — privacy, clarity, bilingual delivery, and operators who need early warning — applied to a focused Canadian product.
